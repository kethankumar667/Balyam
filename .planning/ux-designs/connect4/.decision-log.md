# Connect 4 UX & Visual Identity — Decision Log

## ADR-001: Three Curated Master Themes with Seamless In-Game Switcher
- **Context**: The developer and players require a truly high-premium, rich visual standard that evokes authentic emotion, physical tactile warmth, and high replayability.
- **Decision**: Provide 3 meticulously crafted themes:
  1. `royal_parlour`: Teakwood, brass rivets, lacquered crimson enamel & ivory gold tokens, velvet green table felt.
  2. `cyber_arcade`: Smoked acrylic glass, illuminated tracks, prismatic ruby & electric cyan crystal tokens.
  3. `championship_lounge`: Matte obsidian carbon, 24k brushed gold perimeter, satin black & mirrored gold knurled coins.
- **Status**: Accepted.

## ADR-002: Realistic 3D Token Anatomy & Drop Physics
- **Context**: Flat circles with plain letters feel cheap and digital.
- **Decision**: Render tokens with concentric grip rings, specular light gloss sheens, and theme-specific embossed royal insignias. Animate drops using staged acceleration and micro-rebound bounce.
- **Status**: Accepted.

## ADR-003: Physical Board Pedestal & Live Token Racks
- **Context**: Connect 4 is inherently a physical standing board. Abstract floating holes lack weight.
- **Decision**: Render an authentic base stand and side pillars holding the board upright, accompanied by live token racks displaying each player's remaining disc supply.
- **Status**: Accepted.
